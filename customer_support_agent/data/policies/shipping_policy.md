# NovaTech Audio — Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times
Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days within the country. Express shipping (available at checkout for an extra fee) takes 1-2 business days. International orders typically take 7-14 business days depending on customs processing.

## Order Statuses
- **Processing**: Order received, being prepared for shipment. This stage usually lasts 1 business day.
- **In Transit**: Order has left the warehouse and is on its way to the customer.
- **Delivered**: Order has been delivered to the shipping address.
- **Delayed**: Order is taking longer than expected; customers should be proactively notified.

## Lost or Delayed Packages
If an order shows "In Transit" for more than 10 business days past the estimated delivery date, the customer is eligible to request a reshipment or a full refund without needing to wait for the item to arrive, since it is presumed lost.

## Shipping Costs
Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders under $75 have a flat shipping fee of $5.99 for standard shipping.

## Address Changes
Shipping addresses can only be changed while an order is in "Processing" status. Once an order moves to "In Transit," the address cannot be changed and the customer should be advised to contact the courier directly or arrange a package redirect if the courier supports it.
