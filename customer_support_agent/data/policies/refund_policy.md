# NovaTech Audio — Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window
Customers may request a full refund within 30 calendar days of the delivery date, provided the product is in its original condition with all accessories and packaging included. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

## Defective or Damaged Items
If an item arrives broken, defective, or damaged in transit, the customer is eligible for a full refund OR a free replacement, regardless of the 30-day window, as long as the issue is reported within 60 days of delivery. Customers should provide a photo of the damage when possible, but a photo is not strictly required to open a claim. Shipping costs for defective returns are covered by NovaTech Audio.

## Change of Mind Returns
If a customer simply changes their mind and the product is not defective, the standard 30-day window applies and the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. A 10% restocking fee may apply to opened "change of mind" returns on studio equipment (e.g., NovaBeat Studio Headphones).

## Non-Refundable Situations
Refunds are not available if:
- The delivery window has not yet completed (order status is still "In Transit" or "Processing").
- More than 60 days have passed since delivery, even for defective items — in this case the warranty policy applies instead of a refund.
- The product shows evidence of misuse, unauthorized repair, or water damage not covered by the IPX rating.

## Partial Refunds
Partial refunds may be issued for orders where only part of a multi-item order is defective, or where a minor cosmetic defect does not affect function and the customer prefers to keep the item.

## How to Request a Refund
Support agents should verify order status and delivery date first, confirm the reported issue, and then check refund eligibility before creating a support ticket for the refunds team to process.
