# NovaTech Audio — Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty Coverage
All NovaTech Audio products come with a manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship:
- NovaBeat Wireless Headphones: 12 months
- NovaBuds Pro Earbuds: 12 months
- NovaBoom Portable Speaker: 6 months
- NovaBeat Studio Headphones: 24 months

## What's Covered
The warranty covers battery failure not caused by misuse, driver/speaker failure, button and control malfunctions, charging port defects, and manufacturing defects in materials.

## What's Not Covered
The warranty does not cover accidental damage (drops, crushing), water damage beyond the stated IP rating, damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications, normal wear and tear of ear cushions or tips, or cosmetic scratches that don't affect function.

## Warranty Claims After the Refund Window
If a product develops a covered defect after the 60-day refund/damage window but within its warranty period, the customer is entitled to a free repair or replacement (NovaTech Audio's choice), not a cash refund. The customer should ship the item to the returns center; NovaTech Audio covers return shipping for approved warranty claims.

## Extending Warranty
Customers who register their product within 30 days of purchase on the NovaTech Audio website receive an additional 3 months of warranty coverage at no cost.
